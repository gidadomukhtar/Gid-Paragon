Gid Paragon 

Gid Paragon is a technology company focused on building practical software solutions, delivering software development services, and creating digital products that solve real-world problems.

Our vision is to build useful, accessible, and reliable technology for individuals, businesses, schools, universities, and communities across Africa and beyond.

🌟 What We're Building

Gid Paragon brings software services, product development, and community-driven innovation together under one brand.

💻 Software Development Services

We aim to help clients turn ideas into useful digital solutions, including websites, web applications, business software, and customized technology solutions.

🚀 Digital Products and SaaS

We are developing digital products designed to address practical challenges in education, document verification, student collaboration, and business management.

Our projects include:

- CampusLink: A campus-focused platform concept for student connections, opportunities, and lost-and-found services.
- TrustChain: A digital document verification platform concept exploring document integrity, QR-based verification, cryptographic hashes, and fraud detection.
- SmartStudy: A student-focused learning platform concept for organizing academic resources, assignments, goals, and AI-assisted practice.
- LedgerMind: An expense-tracking solution for small and medium-sized businesses.
- .NET Learning Hub: A learning platform concept for helping developers learn C# and .NET through structured educational resources.

Project availability and feature completeness vary. Refer to each project's current implementation for its working features.
