OUR STORY

More than learning to code. A community built to grow

Technology creates opportunities, but discovering those opportunities and knowing where to begin can be difficult for aspiring developers

ChainDev was initiated by Gidado Mukhtar Balangoggo, founder of Gid Paragon, with a vision of building a connected and supportive developer community.

Through practical learning, collaborative projects, open-source participation, and community activities, ChainDev aims to help learners move from curiosity to building real software.

Starting from our surrounding communities, we want to make it easier for people to learn together, share knowldge, and discover pathways into the technology ecosystem.

OUR MISSION

Make technology more accessible. Make collaboration more powerful. Make growth a shared journey.

We want to create an environment where beginners feel welcome, developers keep growing, and people turn what they learn into things that matter.

OUR VISION

From local communities to the global technology ecosystem

We envision a future where a learner's location does not prevent them from accessing knowledge, building useful software, contributing to open source, or connecting with people who can help them grow.

ChainDev aims to help strengthen the developer ecosystem around FUDMA, Dutsin-Ma, Katsina, Northern Nigeria, and beyond.
